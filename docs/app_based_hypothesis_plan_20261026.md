# App-based hypothesis and figure plan

26 October 2026. This is an analysis and presentation plan, not an interpretation of results. It uses the current app's processed representations together with the corrected cell counts, acquisition-edge eligibility and existing hypothesis measurements. It does not rebuild the app data pipeline. The [original H1–H10 proposal](T1D_research_hypotheses_20261022.md) remains the historical specification; approved method changes and the present endpoint amendments must be identified alongside it.

## Scope and current direction

Use all 20 accepted donors (7 ND, 7 Aab+, 6 T1D); donor 78 remains excluded. Direct disease comparisons are primary. Report the actual conditional association within each disease group separately from its difference between groups. Donors remain the biological replicates.

Do not use mean, median, summed, raw, corrected, standardized or normalized CODEX INS intensity as a quantitative endpoint. Retain the accepted Beta-cell labels for counts, presence, density and composition. H5's original within-cell hormone-content endpoint has no count-only equivalent; its proposed replacement below is a different hypothesis. The proposed H7 Beta-quantity endpoint also changes the particular outcome being tested. Earlier INS-response analyses remain historical outputs. The restriction on INS does not by itself establish a restriction on every other marker; preserve each other marker's actual measurement definition.

Use the app representations actively: the shared composition map and clusters describe endocrine composition states; pseudotime organizes the app's core/peri profiles; physical maps and neighborhood measurements locate those states in tissue. Display the feature definitions alongside them. Their defining composition features may be shown as state descriptions, without claiming that their association independently validates the representation. This does not prevent using the same objects for the separately defined structural, immune and clinical comparisons.

The owner's structural-cell clarification retains the established base-analysis rule: a cell's centroid must lie within the relevant structural object, the required fraction of its area must be contained, and the corresponding marker and highest-marker criteria must be met; cells retaining another identity also have passthrough measurements. The app's choice to display structural object density/area was a display decision to avoid confusion between cell-type density and object density. It did not authorize disabling these base-analysis steps or discarding their measurements. Use the retained Senior-derived implementation and existing inputs to restore these cell measurements offline on the accepted edge-eligible cell universe, including Senior's union-area containment definition. Retain both structural cell counts/percentages/densities and structural object area/density for research, with passthrough measurements separately defined. A structural label being hidden or disabled in the served export does not make its existing computational rule or retained inputs unavailable. No app UI change is part of this figure task.

## Original if–then conditions and execution record

- Keep each original hypothesis's “If supported,” “If a meaningful effect is excluded” and same-data follow-up text linked to the original proposal. Record endpoint/method amendments beside those conditions; do not silently transfer a branch to a different endpoint.
- Report the estimate, interval, test, donor support and technical estimability for each completed comparison. A nonsignificant test does not assign the meaningful-effect-excluded branch. Do not invent effect thresholds after inspecting results.
- Where the original proposal did not specify a numerical threshold or where a changed endpoint needs a new condition, record **branch judgment not assigned**. Continue authorized computation and presentation; that status is not a reason to withhold usable measurements or plots.
- Distinguish conditional association, a direct disease difference and practical model estimability. A null disease interaction alone does not answer whether a conditional association is present; separate within-group significance results do not establish a disease difference.
- Track each original follow-up factually as completed, partially completed, not run, or superseded, with the actual input/output path. No autonomous biological conclusions, mechanism assignments or recommendations accompany the statistical output under the current owner instruction.
