Islet Explorer

An interactive explorer of pancreatic islet CODEX multiplexed imaging data from nPOD donors without diabetes, with islet autoantibodies, and with type 1 diabetes. It compares endocrine composition and the islet microenvironment across donors through linked plots, a trajectory view, spatial neighbourhoods and a whole-slide image viewer.

Laboratory of Maigan Brusko, PhD, University of Florida.

Open the Islet Explorer

Access is restricted to invited reviewers. The browser asks for the username and password you were given; the same sign-in covers the application and its images.

Section-level associations and the orientation of the trajectory do not establish disease progression.
